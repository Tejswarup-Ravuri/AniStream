Shonen

These stories commonly feature themes of friendship, courage, determination, high-stakes battles, and self-improvement

Isakei

The stories commonly feature where a protagonist is transported from their ordinary world to a different or parallel one, such as a fantasy realm, a sci-fi world, or a game-like universe

Sports

Sports anime feature stories centered on sports, often blending athletic competitions with themes of friendship, determination, and teamwork

Comedy

A comedy anime is a style of Japanese animation centered on humor, designed to elicit laughter from its audience

Romance

Romance anime are animated series that explore love and relationships between characters, often focusing on the development of romantic feelings and the challenges they face

Sci-fi

Sci-fi anime is a broad genre that blends science fiction with animation, often featuring futuristic worlds, advanced technology, and space travel
